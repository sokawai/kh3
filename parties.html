Party FAQs

Everything you need to know before you book.

Can I bring my own food? add

Yes — outside food is very welcome. You're free to bring your own catering, a birthday cake, snacks, or light refreshments. We kindly ask guests to tidy up before leaving.

Is stroller parking available? add

Yes, designated stroller parking is available within the building. The space is also elevator-accessible for families who need it.

How many guests can we invite? add

Our base private rental accommodates up to 10 guests total (adults and children combined). Our Birthday Celebration package fits up to 15 guests. For larger groups, reach out and we'll explore a custom arrangement.

Can I reschedule or cancel? add

We understand that plans change with little ones! Please contact us at kenchahouse@gmail.com and we'll do our best to accommodate a reschedule. Full cancellation and rescheduling terms will be shared at booking confirmation.

What ages are parties best suited for? add

Our space is designed for babies and toddlers ages 0–4. Parties are ideal for 1st–4th birthdays, baby showers, and small toddler gatherings.

Do you provide decorations? add

We offer optional styling add-ons for Birthday Celebration packages. The base rental gives you access to our beautifully designed space, which serves as a wonderful backdrop on its own.

How does payment work? add

Once your booking request is confirmed, we'll ask you to send an Interac e-Transfer to kenchahouse@gmail.com within 24 hours to secure your date. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

More questions? See our full FAQ or email us.
