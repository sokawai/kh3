Our Mission

Kencha House was founded with a singular vision: to create a sanctuary where the aesthetic of the home meets the joy of the playground. We believe that children deserve spaces that respect their senses, and parents deserve a moment of reprieve in an environment that feels like a retreat.

Our mission is to foster connection, celebrate early childhood milestones, and provide a curated backdrop for the memories that matter most.

A Note from Sarah

Founder & Creative Director

"As a mother of two, I found myself constantly searching for a space that felt both welcoming to my children and inspiring to me. I wanted a place that didn't compromise on design or tranquility."

"Kencha House is my heart poured into four walls. It’s a place where we slow down, where the coffee is hot, and where play is treated with the reverence it deserves."

The Journey

Spring 2021

The Seed

The first sketches of Kencha House were drawn on napkins at a local park, born from a need for better community spaces.

Winter 2022

Finding Home

We discovered our sun-drenched space on Dupont Street. Renovations began with a focus on natural materials and soft textures.

Autumn 2023

Doors Opening

Kencha House welcomed its first little dreamers. Our community has been growing and flourishing ever since.
