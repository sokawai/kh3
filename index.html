Our Story

"Kencha House was born from a desire to create a sanctuary where children could explore wonder and parents could find a moment of peace."

Inspired by the quiet magic of nature and minimal design, we've created a cafe that feels like a world of its own. We believe that parenting is a little easier when you can just enjoy being in the moment.

Common Inquiries

Everything you need to know before visiting

What are your open-play hours?

We are open Tuesday-Friday 9am-4pm, and Saturdays 8:30am-10:30am. Weekend afternoons are reserved for private celebrations.

Do I need to book in advance?

While walk-ins are welcome if space permits, we highly recommend booking your time slot online to guarantee entry.
